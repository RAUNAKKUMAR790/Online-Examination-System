PROEXAM REAL EMAIL OTP SETUP

1. Install Node.js 18+.
2. Copy .env.example to .env.
3. Configure SMTP_USER, SMTP_FROM and SMTP_PASS with your Gmail address and Google App Password (not normal Gmail password; remove spaces).
4. Run npm.cmd install, then npm.cmd start.
5. Open http://localhost:3000.
6. Test student registration with an email inbox you can access. Registration is not saved until its OTP is verified.
7. Test Forgot Password with that registered email. A second OTP is sent for password reset.

OTP behavior: 6 digits, 10-minute expiry, maximum five verification attempts, and a 60-second resend cooldown. Wrong/expired OTPs do not complete registration or password reset.

Never share .env. It is intentionally excluded from the project ZIP. Real Gmail delivery must be tested on the target computer after installing the actual npm dependencies and configuring a valid App Password.
